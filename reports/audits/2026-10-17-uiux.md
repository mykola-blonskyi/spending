# Audit Report

Date: 2026-10-17

Auditor: Claude (UI/UX audit, read-only)

Audit Type: UI/UX and accessibility audit of `apps/web` (main at 5cd4592)

---

## Scope

The React PWA in `apps/web`. I checked it in a running build, not only in the code:

- Built with `corepack pnpm -r build` (with `verify-deps-before-run=false`, because the default
  deps check wanted to purge `node_modules`).
- Ran `e2e/shots.spec.ts` with `SHOTS_DIR` set:
  - all 8 layouts × light/dark × 390 px/1440 px × 14 screens, in English;
  - the calm layout again in uk, ru and es.
  - That gave 616 screenshots.
- Wrote throwaway Playwright specs outside the repo, using the repo's e2e fixtures and seed
  helpers. They ran at 375 px and covered:
  - empty states for a new user;
  - entry validation, comma decimals and a keyboard tab walk;
  - delete confirmation and the filter/More sheets;
  - offline save, cold offline navigation and sync feedback;
  - a rejected change (category archived on another device);
  - a two-device conflict and its review dialog;
  - CSV import end to end, with mapping, summary, a scanned-PDF error and transfer suggestions;
  - long account and category names in uk, ru and es;
  - a touch-target scan.
- Databases `audit_ui_e2e` and `audit_ui2_e2e` (the second one so the two harness runs could go
  in parallel). Both are dropped. The repo working tree is clean.

Screenshots were taken locally and are not kept.

Not covered:

- the family review page with a second member's queue (it is covered by `review.spec.ts`, but I
  took no screenshot);
- a screen reader;
- real iOS/Android devices. Playwright Chromium has no hyphenation dictionaries, so
  `hyphens-auto` results may differ on phones.

## Findings

Severity levels:

- Critical means data loss or a blocked core task.
- High means a core task is confusing or misleading.
- Medium means a visible defect with a workaround.
- Low means polish.

### Critical

- None found.

### High

- **H1. Saving an invalid entry gives no visible feedback on phones.**
  - Where. All entry screens. `features/transactions/use-entry-form.ts:254-258`.
  - What the user experiences. At 375 px, Save is at the bottom of the form, and the amount field
    and its error ("Enter an amount above zero…") are about 900 px above it. `submit()` sets
    `attempted` and returns without moving focus or scrolling, so tapping Save with an empty
    amount or with "abc" appears to do nothing. The URL stays `/transactions/new`.
  - Fix. When `validate` returns no draft, focus the first invalid field after render. For
    example, `requestAnimationFrame(() => event?.currentTarget.querySelector('[aria-invalid="true"]')?.focus())`.
    The existing `scroll-padding-bottom` already keeps it clear of the bottom bar.

- **H2. The sync center says "Everything is in sync" while changes are pending or have failed.**
  - Where. `/sync`. `features/sync/sync-page.tsx:51-56`, copy at `i18n/messages/en.ts:219`.
  - What the user experiences. `SyncPage` lists only conflicts. Offline with one pending change,
    the header badge reads "Working offline, 1 pending", and the badge links to `/sync`. That page
    then shows "Everything is in sync. Changes you make offline will wait here…", but they never
    appear there. The same happens with "1 failed". Users who follow the badge to find the failed
    change land on a page that contradicts it.
  - Fix. Show the empty state only when there are no conflicts and no pending or failed entries.
    Otherwise add sections that list pending and failed rows, reusing the history rows filtered
    by `sync.state`, each linking to the entry. At minimum, change the empty-state wording and
    show "N waiting / N failed" with a link to history.

- **H3. A "Not synced" or "Waiting to sync" marker hides the entry's title and runs over the
  amount.**
  - Where. History rows in calm (the default layout), split and hybrid, and the split detail
    pane. `features/transactions/layouts/calm-history.tsx:73`, `split-history.tsx:57`,
    `blocks/entry-detail.tsx:283`.
  - What the user experiences. The marker gets `className="shrink-0"`, which cancels its own
    `min-w-0` and `truncate`. A rejected row then shows only the red marker text. The
    description "Market run" is gone and the text spills under "-€12.00". In uk, a pending row's
    title collapses to "Без ...".
  - Fix. Drop `shrink-0`. Either cap the marker (`max-w-[55%]`) or render the failed marker on
    the subtitle line, as cards, soft and journal already do.

- **H4. Transfer suggestions are unreadable on phones.**
  - Where. Import summary. `features/imports/transfer-suggestions.tsx:68-70`.
  - What the user experiences. The row is `flex-wrap`, but its text column is `min-w-0 flex-1`,
    so it shrinks instead of wrapping. At 375 px the description shows as "F…" and "Other side:
    Main, Oct 5, 2026" wraps one word per line beside the buttons. Users cannot tell which entry
    they are confirming as a transfer.
  - Fix. Give the text column a wrap threshold, e.g. `min-w-[12rem] flex-1` (or
    `basis-full sm:basis-auto`), so the amount and buttons drop to a second line.

### Medium

- **M1. The entry screen does not say why a change was rejected.**
  - Where. 6 of 8 layouts. `layouts/calm-entry.tsx:56`, `cards-entry.tsx:158`,
    `bento-entry.tsx:108`, `soft-entry.tsx:114`, `playful-entry.tsx:118`,
    `journal-entry.tsx:406`.
  - What the user experiences. Opening a failed entry shows the form and a "Discard change"
    button, but no reason. In the test the reason was "This category is archived". The archived
    category even appears selected. Only split and hybrid show the reason, through
    `EntryDetail`.
  - Fix. When `form.canDiscard`, render the `<SyncMarker>`, or an `Alert` with the same
    `errors.<code>` text, above the form. A shared block would cover all six layouts.

- **M2. History totals overflow the screen with large amounts in uk and ru.**
  - Where. `features/transactions/blocks/totals-line.tsx:17`.
  - What the user experiences. `<dl className="flex gap-3">` does not wrap. With "Витрачено
    1 234 613,39 EUR Отримано 2 500,00 EUR" the page scrolls sideways (scrollWidth 390 at 375) and
    the Received amount is cut off.
  - Fix. Use `flex flex-wrap gap-x-3 gap-y-1`. Apply the same to `DayNets` (line 60,
    `flex gap-2`), which will overflow with three or more currencies.

- **M3. Category tile labels break mid-word.**
  - Where. Entry category grid. `features/transactions/blocks/entry-fields.tsx:172` and `:210`;
    same in `cards-entry.tsx:122` and `playful-entry.tsx:94`.
  - What the user experiences. At 4 columns × 11 px, `break-words` splits "Entertainmen|t" and
    "Subscription|s" without a hyphen, in English, in light and dark, in the calm and hybrid
    layouts. uk got proper hyphens; Chromium may lack en hyphenation dictionaries.
  - Fix. Either use 3 columns below about 400 px (`grid-cols-3 min-[400px]:grid-cols-4`), or
    replace `break-words` with `[overflow-wrap:normal]` and let `line-clamp-2` plus the existing
    `title` ellipsize.

- **M4. The bento layout puts the Expense/Income/Transfer toggle after the categories.**
  - Where. `features/transactions/layouts/bento-entry.tsx:62-77`.
  - What the user experiences. On phones, users pick a category and only then reach the toggle
    that swaps the category list. Keyboard and screen-reader order is the same (amount,
    categories, kind). Every other layout asks for the kind first.
  - Fix. Move the kind/currency `Tile` before the category `Tile` in the DOM. The `lg:col/row`
    placement keeps the desktop grid unchanged.

### Low

- **L1. A cold offline start shows skeletons for about 3 s before the offline message.**
  - Where. Dashboard, budgets, recurring and imports. `data/query-client.ts:6-9`.
  - What the user experiences. TanStack's `onlineManager` starts "online", so network errors are
    retried twice before `QueryUnavailable` can show its offline state. At 0.8 s the dashboard
    still showed skeletons.
  - Fix. In `shouldRetry`, return `false` when `!navigator.onLine`, or call
    `onlineManager.setOnline(navigator.onLine)` at boot.

- **L2. Offline, the imports list shows a generic error.**
  - Where. `features/imports/imports-page.tsx:121-126`.
  - What the user experiences. "No connection to the server… Retry" appears under the line
    "Importing needs a connection. Your past imports are listed below". Other REST screens show a
    calm offline empty state instead.
  - Fix. Use `QueryUnavailable` here, like budgets and recurring do.

- **L3. The file picker is not translated.**
  - Where. `features/imports/new-import-page.tsx:596`.
  - What the user experiences. The native "Choose file / No file chosen" text follows the
    browser's language, not the app's, so it stays English in a uk/ru/es session on an English
    browser.
  - Fix. Visually hide the input and add a translated `<Label>` styled as a button, plus the
    chosen file name.

- **L4. The import copy omits PDF.**
  - Where. `i18n/messages/en.ts:206` and `:526`, and the same keys in uk/ru/es.
  - What the user experiences. "Upload a CSV or XLSX…" leaves out PDF, which the hint on the same
    page and the parser support.
  - Fix. "Upload a CSV, XLSX or PDF statement…" in all four catalogs.

- **L5. The journal layout's search focus indicator is too weak.**
  - Where. `features/transactions/layouts/journal-history.tsx:99`.
  - What the user experiences. The input is `outline-none`, and the only indicator is a 1 px
    bottom border changing colour. That likely fails WCAG 2.4.11 and 1.4.11. Other layouts use a
    `focus-within:ring-[3px]`.
  - Fix. Add `focus-within:ring-[3px] focus-within:ring-ring/50` or a 2 px border.

- **L6. The reduced-motion rule makes the sync spinner jitter.**
  - Where. `shared/styles.css:323-330`.
  - What the user experiences. The rule sets `animation-duration: 0.01ms` but keeps `infinite`
    iterations, so `animate-spin` on "Syncing" redraws at random angles every frame.
  - Fix. Add `animation-iteration-count: 1 !important`.

- **L7. Category icons have low contrast in dark mode.**
  - Where. `shared/components` CategoryBadge, on calm and soft tiles.
  - What the user experiences. Tinted icons on dark tinted circles (Other, Subscriptions) are
    barely visible.
  - Fix. Raise the icon lightness in the dark tokens, or put the badge on a `bg-card` ring.

- **L8. The "Manage budgets" link is a small touch target.**
  - Where. `features/dashboard/blocks/budget-overview.tsx:37`.
  - What the user experiences. The link is 20 px tall, the only target under 24 px that the scan
    found.
  - Fix. Use `inline-flex min-h-11 items-center`, or `Button variant="link"`.

### Suggestions (not defects)

- **S1. Empty dashboard.** A new user sees three €0.00 tiles, an empty chart and "+€0.00 vs
  previous period" with no next step. Show the history empty-state CTA ("Add transaction / Import
  statement") when the summary has no entries.
- **S2. Delta against an empty period.** "+€649.30 vs previous period" when the previous period is
  empty is noise. Hide the delta, or say "No data for September", when the previous total is 0.
- **S3. Zero unconverted amounts.** The unconverted line prints "Received CHF 0.00". Skip zero
  sides there.
- **S4. Undo import button.** It has no pending state. Disable it and show a spinner while
  `undo.isPending`.
- **S5. Journal currencies.** The journal entry offers only 6 currency pills (CHF, EUR, GBP, PLN,
  UAH, USD). Check whether other account currencies are reachable there.

## Areas found sound

- **Destructive actions.** Every one goes through `ConfirmDialog`. That covers delete entry,
  archive account or category, undo import, discard change, family leave/remove, and sign out
  with a "Sync now" alternative. Account deletion requires typing a phrase. Focus returns to the
  opener.
- **Conflict review.** The dialog is clear at 375 px. Differing fields are highlighted, radios
  work with the keyboard, and the description names the fields.
- **Import mapping.** The preview table scrolls inside its own container with no page overflow.
  The scanned-PDF error is specific. The summary focuses its heading.
- **Empty states.** History, imports, budgets, recurring and family have well-written empty
  states with actions, in all four languages.
- **Offline feedback.** The banner, the badge ("Working offline, 1 pending") and the row marker
  are all clear, and REST screens show proper offline messages after retries.
- **Keyboard.** A skip link is present. Tab order on the entry form is logical. Kind and category
  groups are roving radio groups. Search fields have `sr-only` labels, and the filter button has a
  counted `aria-label`.
- **Forms.** Errors are tied to inputs with `aria-invalid` and `aria-describedby`. A comma decimal
  ("12,50") is accepted.
- **Number, currency and date formatting.** It follows CLDR per locale. uk uses "EUR", es has no
  grouping for four digits, ru adds "г.". This is expected, not a bug.
- **Translations.** uk, ru and es are consistent with the glossary. I saw no wrong or
  untranslated strings on the screens I captured.
- **Long strings.** Long account and category names truncate cleanly in rows and pickers, and uk
  hyphenates.
- **Dark mode.** All 8 layouts render coherently, and balances and status colours stay legible.
- **Reduced motion.** A global rule exists, apart from L6.
- **Desktop.** The sidebar layouts, split master-detail and journal sentence entry have no
  overflow or layout breaks.

## Recommendations

### Immediate

- H1. Focus the first invalid field on submit.
- H3. Drop `shrink-0` from `SyncMarker` in the calm, split and detail rows.
- H4. Add a wrap threshold to the transfer suggestion text column.

### Short Term

- H2. List pending and failed changes in the sync center, or fix its empty state.
- M1. Show the rejection reason on the entry screens.
- M2. Make the totals line and `DayNets` wrap.
- M3. Fix the category tile word breaking.
- M4. Reorder the bento kind toggle.

### Long Term

- Add an E2E overflow check at 375 px with uk strings and large amounts. `overflow.spec.ts`
  exists; extend it to history totals and failed rows.
- Fix the Low items in one pass (L1, L2, L5, L6, L8).

---

## Overall Assessment

The UI is strong for a personal app:

- consistent shared blocks across 8 layouts;
- careful accessibility in the shared components;
- good empty, offline and conflict handling;
- clean translations.

The real problems sit around sync status and phone-width layout. The sync center contradicts the
badge (H2), failed and pending markers hide the row (H3), and the entry screen gives no reason for
a rejection (M1). In the two main flows, invalid saves are silent on phones (H1) and transfer
suggestions are cramped (H4). Each fix is a few lines.
